Frequently asked questions

Do you use AI?

Yes, and we are good at it. AI tools take over the routine, which is why we are fast and affordable. What the site says, how it is structured and how it looks is decided by a designer.

How are you this fast?

A short brief instead of weeks of meetings, a worked-out process, and tools we know inside out. A landing page takes 72 hours, a multi-page website 7 days, an online store 2 weeks.

What do you need from me?

The completed brief, your logo and photos if you have them, and a couple of sites you like.

I have no texts. Is that a problem?

No. We write them from your brief. This is our strong side: we know marketing and how people read a page. That is exactly why we ask you to fill in the brief carefully: the better we understand your business, your customers and your offer, the more precisely the texts will sell.

What if I want changes?

Every package includes 2 rounds of changes. One round is a complete list of everything you want to fix, sent to us in a single file. If changes arrive as many separate messages, each message counts as a round, so only the first two are included.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Spanish and Russian.

How does payment work?

We work with a 50% upfront payment: it books your slot and we start right away. The remaining 50% is paid when all the work is done, before launch. We accept PayPal, Stripe, USDT and Mercado Pago.

Do you only make content, or run the account too?

Both. Strategy, profile setup, content and account management are separate services. Take one, or all of them together.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay for the setup and management of your ads; the ad budget goes directly to the platform.

How do we start?

Write to us on WhatsApp or Telegram. We’ll talk about your business and goals and tell you the scope, price and timeline.
